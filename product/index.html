FAQ

Everything you want to know about data, accounts and purchasing.

Where is my data stored? Is it safe?
All data lives only in your own private iCloud database (Apple CloudKit). The developer cannot read or export it. There is no account system and no third-party analytics or ad SDK.
Do I need to sign up?
No. Just download and start — sign in to iCloud and syncing plus family sharing work automatically.
Is it free?
The core diary is free. A subscription unlocks the full toolkit: unlimited history and trends, doctor-visit PDF reports, Excel export, family sharing and health summaries.
Which devices are supported?
iPhone and iPad, each with a tailored layout, plus Apple Watch for quick logging. Data syncs via iCloud across your devices.
